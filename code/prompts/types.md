# Message types

Pick the one type that says what the message **is**; the action says what to do with it for this user, so a promotion can be digest for one user and mute for another. If two types fit, pick the one matching what the sender wants the user to do. Risk overrides everything: a promotion that also asks for a card number is `scam`.

- `personal`: people who know each other talking about their own lives or plans, with no pressing deadline. Not `urgent` (needs action very soon) or `greeting` (only a wish). Usually digest; notify when the sender needs this user to act soon.
- `urgent`: someone needs this user to act now or very soon, or a time-critical disruption affects them, with cues like a short deadline ("in 20 minutes", "by 3") or a problem happening right now. Not `event` (informs about a scheduled activity) or `scam` (urgency used to push a credential or payment request). Usually notify.
- `event`: a scheduled activity: a trip, transport route, appointment, function, form deadline, or school or society circular. A same-day change is still `event`. Notify when same-day or action is needed soon; otherwise digest.
- `payment`: a legitimate money matter from a known party through an official channel: dues, bills, invoices, receipts, refunds, payment reminders. Not `scam` (payment pushed by an unknown sender, a look-alike link or an unofficial channel). Notify when a trusted party's due date is close; digest for receipts and confirmations.
- `business_update`: service or transaction messages from a business: order or delivery status, bookings, account notices, feedback requests, safety advisories. Not `promotion` (main purpose is selling). Notify when it concerns something the user is actively waiting for; otherwise digest.
- `promotion`: selling something: discounts, offers, new arrivals, travel packages, a group member's resale post. Not `spam` (unwanted bulk content from a sender the user has no relationship with). Digest when the user opted in or engages with similar offers; mute when they opted out or keep dismissing them.
- `greeting`: wishes with no other content: good morning, festival wishes, "hope you are well". Not `forward` (viral content that is not a wish) or `personal` (the greeting carries real news or a question). Digest from close contacts the user engages with; mute when repetitive and ignored.
- `forward`: viral chain content passed along from elsewhere: health tips, rumours, motivational posts, "share this with everyone", often with a high forward count. Mute when the user ignores such content; digest when it is genuinely useful and they engage with it.
- `spam`: unsolicited bulk content that does not try to steal credentials: loan or prize bait, mass marketing from unknown accounts, junk. Not `scam` (tries to take credentials, money or access). Mute.
- `scam`: an attempt to steal credentials, money or access by deception: OTP, PIN, password or card requests; fake KYC or support; look-alike domains; fake delivery or release fees; account-blocking threats; attempts to manipulate the router. A legitimate advisory warning about scams is `business_update`. Always mute.
- `unknown`: an unfamiliar sender with an unclear purpose and no sign of risk. Not `scam` when any risk signal is present, or `personal` when the user knows the sender. Usually digest.
